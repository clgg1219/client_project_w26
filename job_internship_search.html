Job and Internship Searching and Planning

How do I start planning to search for internships?

You are a student, and you know that internships are an invaluable aspect of starting a career, so you would like to start searching for internships. However, the prospect of starting this process is daunting (and understandably so)! Fortunately, planning can help you develop a focused approach to identifying what you are looking for in an internship, as well as help you gather all your materials to apply and then track your applications.

Here are some things to think about before starting the search:

  • What industry(ies) do I want to work in?
  • Do I want a remote, in-person, or hybrid internship? Why?
  • What are my values?
  • What do I value in a workplace; what is my ideal workplace culture?

Then, after thinking the above questions through, your materials can be prepared for the search. These can be as follows:

  • A spreadsheet tracking the statuses of all your applications
  • A well-formatted and updated resume
  • A cover letter (this is not mandatory, though some postions may require one)
  • An elevator pitch (if you plan on attending a career fair)

Where Do I Search for Internships?

So, after planning, you are ready to start searching and applying for jobs. However, you are not sure where to start. No worries, there are plenty of resources for you to find roles, both in-person and online!

  • LinkedIn
  • Handshake
  • UMSI Careerlink
  • Career fairs- they are hosted throughout the year
